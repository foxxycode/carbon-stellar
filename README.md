# CarbonStellar

CarbonStellar is a decentralized carbon credit trading platform that revolutionizes environmental impact measurement through community-verified carbon sequestration projects. The platform introduces a novel "Proof of Impact" consensus mechanism where local communities act as validators for carbon offset projects in their regions, earning governance tokens for accurate verification. Unlike traditional carbon markets that rely on centralized certification bodies, CarbonStellar creates real-time, tamper-proof carbon impact measurements through distributed community validation.

The platform leverages a hybrid oracle system that combines satellite data feeds with ground-level community verification to create a trustless yet human-verified impact measurement system. Smart contracts automatically release payments based on verified milestones, while a dynamic pricing algorithm adjusts carbon credit values based on local environmental urgency scores. The technical infrastructure integrates IoT sensors, satellite imagery, and community reporting to enable fractional carbon credit ownership and automated reinvestment of trading fees into high-impact projects.

CarbonStellar enables small-scale participants to pool resources for larger environmental projects while allowing users to offset personal emissions through micro-purchases of community-verified credits. The platform creates a circular economy where successful project completion increases token value while funding new environmental initiatives ranging from reforestation to regenerative agriculture. Community members can propose and vote on new carbon sequestration initiatives with funding automatically distributed through milestone-based smart contracts, ensuring continuous environmental benefit scaling.
